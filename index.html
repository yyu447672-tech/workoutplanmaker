Questions before you start

Is this workout plan free?

Yes. The assessment, macro calculator and print option are free, with no account or email required.

Are my body details uploaded?

No. Calculations run in your browser, with answers held only in this tab. This site uses Google Analytics for visits and interactions. Custom events contain only a fixed tool name, never your body details or results.

How personalised is the plan?

It matches your answers to transparent formulas and a fixed training-template library. It does not assess injuries, movement quality or training history and is not a personal coach.

Can I change my answers or keep the result?

Yes. Go back to edit an answer, then complete the assessment again. Use Print / Save PDF to keep your result on your own device.

Can the assessment replace medical advice?

No. For a medical condition, injury, pregnancy, nursing or a history of disordered eating, get individual professional guidance.
